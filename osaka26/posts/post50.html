ANOTHER SONG!
I have another song for you. Song making is truly an addiction for me. I suddenly just want to stop doing everything and continue making songs. Every morning, my first instinct is to go on my phone and listen to my latest song. Then, I go to my computer and make more songs. I LOVE IT!

This week I’ve been going on Instagram a lot and at the end of every session, I find myself obsessively scrolling through the page of some model, or comedic personality, or a mix of both.
