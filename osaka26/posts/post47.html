I WENT TO KYOTO


2026.05.23

Hello blog Today, I went to the Kyoto with Hanni-san and Raffaele-san. We visited many temples. We ate a plenty of ice cream that Hanni-san bought for us. It really felt like she was our aunt. I really liked Kyoto. The people seem more real there than Osaka. It almost felt like the Chicago of Japan. Tokyo = New York, Osaka = LA, Kyoto = Chicago. Next time I stay in Japan, I’m gonna stay in Kyoto instead. I loved the vibes of Kyoto. It’s like how I thought Japan would feel as a child. Kyoto station was also a big highlight since it was genuinely breathtaking. I’m gonna go back. Today’s highlights were eating curry from this cute little cafe, and going into one of the temples and praying to wooden Buddha statues. In the temple, it smelt like a the waiting areas for Disney rides. I love that smell. I’ll show photos tomorrow. Anyway, I know I said I was gonna input my short film reviews today, but I did not. I’m tired. I want to watch instagram. Ok, じゃね。
