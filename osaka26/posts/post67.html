SOME DREAM I WROTE ABOUT ON APRIL 6TH, 2019
“ I had this dream where a snake girl fell in love with this man, they met at a subway station, and they ended up falling in love. But when the man sees that the snake girl was grabbing a sword, he thought that she was going to kill the mayor person because of something, so the man kills the mayor person first, but the snake lady person wasn’t trying to kill the mayor, and the man also stabs the lady. The lady went to the hospital, and she said that she is going to make it alive. This one dude hates the lady, and threatens the man that he is going to kill her, but the man tries to stop him, but the man went and killed the lady already. I was like “that’s kinda dumb”

I had sort of liked this snake girl. She was like really scary, but I fell in love with her. She had this unimaginable thing to her that made me feel something, but I don’t know what. The man was really handsome too. The snake girl was part human and part monster or snake. SHE MADE ME SO SCARED THOUGH BUT IT WAS LIKE I WANTED TO STAY THERE. It was a movie I was watching. I just remember this one part where the man like “I can tell you are a monster because of your armpits. Put on deodorant” that part came from george. I am still wanting that feeling from that dream, it was amazing, and I have never felt it in my entire life. I was also sometimes viewing from the mans perspective and 3rd perspective. It was like a feeling a love and seeing someone beautiful in your mind, feeling scared, and also getting your crush to love you all at the same time.

I had another dream where pepper was on the other side of the lake, but the lake was different, it was where we couldn’t get around the lake, and the lake was surrounded by big weeds and stuff like that. And the lake was a straight rectangle. Just a gigantic rectangle. Pepper jumped in the water and successfully doggy paddled for like 2 seconds, and then fell down in the water. She got all the way across the gigantic rectangular lake, but she was all the way down in water. I tried to jump in and rescue her, but I couldn’t get down at all.

Now I’m thinking about the song “down” [by Marian Hill] “
