NOT BLACK ENOUGH…
のずむさん told me he was learning more black slang and he wanted me to teach him something. Disappointingly, I had to tell him that I’m not really culturally black so I wouldn’t know that much. Because of this, I’m now more aware of how little I am in touch with my roots, and that’s okay too. He told me that later when I start having kids is when I’ll think about it.

I will say that it was strange when he was telling me all the black slang he’s learned and saying “you guys use ‘booty’ a lot, right?” It really felt like I was being grouped into something that I have no association to, like putting a dumpling in ravioli. Sure, we are fundamentally the same and made of similar ingredients, but I don’t really know the ravioli culture and what sauces they enjoy being doused in. I am a dumpling so I only know the way of the dumpling. Maybe one day I will learn more about ravioli. This is what it’s like being Maya Alba BTW. I’m gonna turn this into a book: dumpling in raviolis.
