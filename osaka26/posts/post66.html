It wasn’t inspired by anything in particular other than wanting to practice the new synths I have attained from Ableton 12 suite. I used sampler, which turns an audio sample into a modifiable synth. I used it to create a Reese bass which was surprisingly simple. Basically, you take a sample of anything and loop a tiny part of it to the point where it sounds like a bass. Then, I added OTT and saturation (which I actually don’t know what it does other than make things sound good?) I’ve learned that I really don’t know that much about sound design. I basically just flip knobs until something sounds fine, or watch a Youtube tutorial. I want to know what I’m doing and why. That’s the only way I’ll be a successful producer. That being said, every sound in this song (other than the vocal and shaker) I created utilizing synths. Call this my first attempt serious mixing and sound design.
