THANK’S, CHRISTINA!
I have been more comfortable with the fact that we are more than how we are initially perceived. I’ve spent so long thinking about how I want others to see me instead of what role I play in the universe. You know what, I’ve been listening to a lot of 2000-2010 music, including Christina Grimmie. This week, someone said something about Christina Grimmie and I was like “wait.. that name rings a bell…” Yup. I’m sure at some point in my childhood, I knew her + her story. If you don’t know who she is, she’s a singer songwriter who sung pop song covers + made her own music. She’s so cool, like the girl I imagine when I think of the 2010’s. She got shot at a meet and greet, then died the same day. It was pretty big since she was only 22, super talented, super kind and beautiful, probably the last person you expect to be murdered. I’m pretty sure I was affected by this as a yougin’ because when I remembered, I felt childishly sad. As a semi-adult, I don’t really feel sad the same way my child self did maybe because I have more access to things including mental awareness. For example, every time I’m done hanging out with my family now, I’m don’t want to cry anymore; I instead feel satisfied. I can make more sense about the unfortunate things that occur. Though, this Christina Grimmie event brought me right back to the times I’d feel so, so much. Anyway, I really love her music + her style so I’ve been listening a lot. I’m really inspired by her.
