NEW SONG!

This song was inspired by yesterday when I sent my brother my song and he didn’t like it. It got me thinking about all the times when my brother doesn’t like or approve of what I do, and I feel like I semi-failed. It’s called validation, and I most often seek it out of my brother even though we both enjoy different styles of music. I thought this was an interesting aspect, so that’s why I made this song.

The first part is just fun. Maybe it’s like when I’m in the process of making a song and it’s all fun and creative juices flowing. Then, I hear the feedback from my brother which goes into the second part, sort of competitive.

Recently, I’ve been implementing more human sounding elements. It really makes a difference emotionally.
