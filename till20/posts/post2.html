few things on my mind
one being how much my thoughts have changed since being back from Japan. When I came back to the US, one of the first things I noticed is how stressed and aware everyone seemed. now, I fear I am in a similar situation. I am hyper-aware of situations. In Japan, it felt as if there were less stakes in my actions; I screwed up my Starbucks order? oh well, I’ll just try again tomorrow. I walked around with my zipper down? no one cares anyway. I told my teacher I want to marry her accidentally (true story)? haha, that’s a silly story. but now it feels like if I mess up, I’m going to be burned at the stakes. in fact, i’ve become more scared to talk to people in the case that I’m going to say something embarrassing and I’ll end up sitting in the windowsill of some lighthouse, staring out at the grey sky thinking of this regretful moment forever like a widow, but it’s not my husband who died; it was my ego (this is the most poetic sentence i’ve ever written. feel free to text me how this sentence affected your life.) maybe it’s because i’m around my family more and I care about their opinion more than my own? maybe it’s because i’ve generally become more fearful of things? idk. i’ll figure it out. the next thing on my mind is the posting schedule for this tab. genuinely blog, i don’t know if i will be able to do everyday because of how volatile my situation is. in Japan, i had a consistent schedule which made things more manageable. now, I don’t know where I’m going to end up the next second. it’s difficult for sure, but I will try to post everyday. :D lastly, I’m thinking about how scared I am. i was scared to get a job. i’m scared to meet new people. i’m scared of getting older. i am scared of who i might become. i am generally a fearful person. that’s kinda who i’ve been for most of my life. the thing that I’m failing to realize is that I am continually choosing to uphold this narrative of being a fearful, shy person simply because i’m familiar with it. maybe that’s ok. but maybe it’s not because i might want to be a different person at some point in my life.
