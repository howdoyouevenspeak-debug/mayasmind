I LOVE THIS GAME!
Okay blog. I am really full right now and I need to work on my farms in Fields of Mistria. Actually, my brother and I played this game called “Project Zomboid” for a long time yesterday and today. It’s sandbox game about trying to survive a zombie apocalypse. This game was made by freaking geniuses because there are so many different factors of survival in this game, from tracking your insulin, to needing to find pens to mark things on your map, to controlling the heat in your car. I’ve never played a game so close to live before which makes it an epic zombie apocalypse game. The most remarkable part about this for me is that it was released in 2013. They didn’t have AI or advanced technology, and they still managed to create such a jam-packed, high quality sandbox. It’s a solid game. I really like it and it literally only takes up 9 gigs which is bonkers. You could play this on a macbook. There are seriously so, so many elements to this game, it’s crazy.
